How to Use the Roster
Kayachikitsa Duty Roster — Complete Guide
① First-Time Setup
  1. Open the Members section in the left sidebar.
  2. Add each doctor's name under their year group — 3rd Year 2nd Year 1st Year. Type the name and press Enter or click + Add.
  3. To remove a doctor, click × on their name tag.
  4. Member data is saved automatically and carries over every month — you only need to do this once.
② Creating the Monthly Roster
  1. In Settings, select the Month.
  2. Enter any Custom Holidays as day numbers separated by commas — e.g. 5, 15, 26. National holidays (Republic Day, Holi, Diwali etc.) are added automatically.
  3. Click 📋 Create Table — this builds the blank roster grid.
  4. Click ⚡ Auto-Fill — duties are distributed automatically following all department rules.
③ Editing Duties After Auto-Fill
Assign / Change a duty: Click any duty cell → a popup appears → select a doctor → click ✓ Confirm. Click ✕ to cancel without changes.
Clear a slot: Hover over a filled cell → click the ✕ button that appears on the right side of the chip.
Lock a slot: Hover → click 🔒. Locked slots are never overwritten by auto-fill. Click 🔓 to unlock anytime.
Swap two duties: Click ⇄ Swap Duties in the top bar → click the first slot → click the second slot. They swap instantly. Both become manual. Press Cancel to exit.
Double duty (same person, evening + night): Fully allowed manually. In the dropdown, already-assigned doctors show ⚡ Double duty. Select them normally — a warning toast appears but the duty is saved.
Re-running auto-fill: Safe to run anytime. It only refills empty slots and previous auto-filled slots. Manual, DMS, and locked slots are always preserved.
④ DMS Mode

Toggle DMS: ON in the top bar before assigning duties directed by the DMS. Those slots appear in purple on screen and are printed in bold in exported PDF and Word files so they are clearly marked.

⑤ Leave Management
  1. Open Leave Management in the sidebar.
  2. Select the doctor, enter leave dates — e.g. 5-8 for a range, or 12, 14 for individual days.
  3. Click Add. The doctor is automatically skipped during auto-fill on those days.
  4. If a leave overlaps an already-assigned duty, a red conflict warning appears — clear that slot manually.
  5. Remove individual days with × on the date pill, or clear all leaves for a doctor at once.
⑥ Distribution Summary

After auto-fill, the 📊 Distribution panel opens automatically showing each doctor's tally:

🌙 Nights 🌆 Evenings 🌅 Mornings Σ Total

Bad nights (Saturday nights, nights before holidays) are shown in pink (✗) next to the night count so you can spot any imbalance at a glance.

⑦ Exporting
↓ PDF — A4 printable roster with hospital header, highlighted Sundays/holidays, footnotes, and HOD signature line.
↓ Word — .doc file in the same format, editable in Microsoft Word.
↓ Excel — .xlsx spreadsheet with the full roster and footer text.
⑧ Year-End Rotation (When Batch Changes)
  1. Open Members and click 📅 Year-End Rotation.
  2. A preview shows: 3rd year graduates (removed), 2nd year moves to 3rd, 1st year moves to 2nd, and 1st year is cleared.
  3. Click ✅ Confirm Rotation.
  4. Then add the new incoming 1st year batch using + Add under 1st Year.
⑨ Saving & Loading

Everything auto-saves in your browser as you work. If you close and reopen the file, click ↩ Load Draft in the top bar to restore your last session. Click Reset only if you want to start completely fresh — this cannot be undone.
